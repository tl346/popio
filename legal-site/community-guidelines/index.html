Community Guidelines

Effective and last updated July 29, 2026

Popio is for discovering local experiences and sharing useful, genuine information. These Guidelines apply to profiles, events, photos, reviews, chats, and every other interaction on Popio.

Be respectful

Do not harass, bully, threaten, stalk, shame, or target others. Hate speech or attacks based on protected characteristics are prohibited. Do not encourage violence, self-harm, exploitation, or dangerous conduct.

Keep content appropriate

Do not post pornography, sexual exploitation, graphic violence, illegal goods or services, scams, malware, or content that promotes criminal activity. Never post sexual content involving minors. Report immediate danger to local emergency services.

Be authentic and accurate

Submit real events with accurate locations, schedules, descriptions, and images. Do not impersonate people or organizations, fabricate events, manipulate engagement, spam chats, or repeatedly post misleading information.

Respect privacy

Do not share another person's private information, precise live location, private communications, financial information, identification documents, or intimate images without permission. Obtain consent before posting identifiable photos where consent would reasonably be expected.

Respect intellectual property

Only upload content you created or have permission to use. Do not infringe copyrights, trademarks, publicity rights, or other rights. Contact popioadmin@gmail.com with a clear description of claimed infringement and the relevant content.

No manipulation or evasion

Do not create accounts to evade blocks, suspensions, or moderation decisions. Do not coordinate false reports, automate activity without permission, or attempt to access another user's account.

Events and real-world safety

Do not use Popio to organize illegal, deceptive, or foreseeably dangerous events. Event creators must not misrepresent affiliation, admission terms, accessibility, safety requirements, or venue permission. Users should verify event details and use appropriate caution.

Reporting, blocking, and enforcement

Use the in-app report option on events, photos, chats, or profiles that may violate these Guidelines. You can block a user to hide their content. Reports are reviewed by administrators. Depending on severity and history, Popio may reject or remove content, issue warnings, restrict features, suspend or terminate accounts, preserve evidence, or contact appropriate authorities. Egregious violations may result in immediate removal.

Contact

For safety or moderation concerns, use Contact Support in the app or email popioadmin@gmail.com.
